Problem definition
A double crisis with one overlooked solution

⛽ The fuel crisis

Bangladesh imports 95% of its fuel. Middle East disruptions in 2022–2024 drained foreign reserves, sent CNG prices surging, and left 500,000+ drivers without affordable fuel overnight. No local alternative exists.

🌿 The weed crisis

Water hyacinth covers 30%+ of Bangladesh’s navigable waterways. It costs millions to clear annually, blocks river freight and ferry routes, and collapses aquatic ecosystems. Economic value today: zero.

Bangladesh is simultaneously dependent on a fuel it cannot afford to import — and sitting on a free biological feedstock it has never converted to energy. BioNode closes that gap.

Current solutions
Why everything that exists today fails

Imported fossil CNG

Fully dependent on foreign exchange and global supply chains. One geopolitical shock collapses the system — as Bangladesh experienced repeatedly since 2022.

Rural biogas plants

Produce dilute, low-pressure gas for cooking only. Require daily manual supervision. Cannot compress to vehicle-grade pressure. Zero connection to water hyacinth feedstock.

Industrial biogas upgrading

Exists globally but demands multi-crore CAPEX, specialist operators, and equipment through 3-month Letter of Credit cycles Bangladesh’s dollar crisis has made unviable.

No solution in Bangladesh today combines autonomous AI control + on-site gas compression + zero-cost local feedstock in a single deployable container. That is the exact gap BioNode fills.

Role of AI
The AI is not a feature — it is the operating condition
Without the AI, BioNode requires a trained biochemist on-site multiple times per day — making autonomous terminal deployment, 24/7 operation, and Energy-as-a-Service financially impossible.

What the AI monitors (real-time)

pH level · digester temperature · internal pressure · gas composition (CH₄ %, H₂S ppm, CO₂ %) · compressor load · battery state from aquavoltaic array

What the AI controls via PLC

Solenoid valves · heating and cooling elements · feed pump rate · compressor stage activation · load-shifting between solar array, battery, and grid

The Edge-AI inference engine runs embedded on local hardware. No cloud, no internet required. Decisions happen in milliseconds, locally. The system keeps running even when connectivity fails.
