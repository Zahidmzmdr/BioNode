Why it's different

Built for a problem no import can solve.

Four things happening at once that no other system in Bangladesh combines.

⚡

Autonomous by design

An embedded AI monitors pH, temperature, and gas composition continuously, replacing the trained operator a digester normally needs.

🌿

Feedstock that costs nothing

Water hyacinth is a free, abundant nuisance weed — BioNode turns the cost of clearing it into the cost of making fuel.

📦

One container, full process

Digestion, scrubbing, separation, and compression all happen on-site — no pipeline, no trucking, no separate facility.

🔧

Built entirely in Dhaka

Every component is sourced from Nawabpur and fabricated in Dholaikhal — no import cycle, no Letter-of-Credit delay.

How it works

From canal to cylinder in four stages.

1

Anaerobic digestion

Water hyacinth is broken down by bacterial cultures at 35–38°C, producing raw biogas on site.

2

H₂S scrubbing

An iron sponge filter strips hydrogen sulfide to under 10ppm before the gas moves downstream.

3

CO₂ separation

A membrane stage removes CO₂, raising methane purity above 95% — the threshold for vehicle fuel.

4

Compression & dispensing

Multi-stage compression brings the gas to 200 bar, filled directly into standard CNG cylinders for sale.
